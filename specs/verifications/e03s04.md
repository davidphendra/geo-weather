## Deviation discovered during TDD

Polly rejects `HttpRetryStrategyOptions.MaxRetryAttempts < 1` ("must be between 1 and 2147483647"). The plan's
"`0` disables retries" is therefore implemented by **not registering a retry strategy** when the value is `0`;
the `[Range(0, 10)]` bound and a code comment capture this. Recorded in ADR-0015 and the story spec §18.
